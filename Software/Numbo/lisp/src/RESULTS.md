## Gaps investigated

- **#11 is easier for the port than the chapter suggests.**
  - The mechanism is the chapter's. No pnode can propose 16 + 25: the only plus pnode past 10 is `plus5-10`. In the traced seed 14 run, block 41 = 16 + 25 comes from `look-for-new-block`, the random pairing start.l hangs every 5 iterations, at iteration 16.
  - How often that background codelet wins against Pnet-driven codelets depends on the coderack's selection rule. That rule is RECONSTRUCTED (item 5: P = urgency / total urgency), and the chapter gives no numbers for #11. So this is a difference of degree; no porting bug was found. Median 176 iterations against 25–45 for the "obvious" puzzles does show the slower, random search.
- **#6** is solved 1/20. The chapter makes no Numbo claim for it.
- **#3** is solved 2/20 here (3/100 over seeds 1–100, item 10), and the chapter's run failed. This is consistent. Item 10 found two 1987 behaviours, reported in the "invalid Done" and "error" columns: the `kill-block` gap and the `reactivate-cyto` race.
- **The 1987 trace** (trace3.31, puzzle 3) is compared event by event in `src/PORTING_NOTES.md`, "Item 11". The kinds of event, naming, pairing invariants and opening posts match. The port posts many more `look-for-bl+` codelets. Those posts come from codelets.l/init.l code and parameters, and those files postdate the trace.
