# python/seekwhence/gui

The PySide6 GUI: `python3 -m seekwhence.gui` (or `seekwhence-gui`). Each view is an
observer drawing one of the Qt-free models in [`models/`](models/), and the run waits after
every event until every view has redrawn. Every convention follows the Numbo GUI
(`Software/Numbo/python/numbo/gui/`; `ralph_loops/loop0004/ANSWERS.md` 7); the choices the
design leaves open are in [`../../GUI-DECISIONS.md`](../../GUI-DECISIONS.md).

The engine never imports this package: the engine and the CLI need only the standard
library, and a run gives the same events with or without the GUI (the loop's gate checks
both on every iteration).

|---|---|

| `paint.py` | Numbo's `paint.py`: `paint_all`, one window paint per event, each view exactly once. |
| `sequence_view.py` | The sequence pane, P1 (item 03): the term cells with their fate, the fresh hypothesis's block as ghost cells under their positions, the input box (one term or several, queued), the `GuessPanel` (OK / No) and the answer badges (G-014, G-015). |
| `cytoplasm_view.py` | The cytoplasm pane, P2 (items 05 and 06), after Numbo's `tree_view.py`: glints as teardrops on a baseline, bonds as arcs below it coloured by type and labelled with their values under reformulation, sparks as dashed arcs that flash and fade, as Ch. 3 Figure 1 [M86 p. 102] and FCCA's glue [FCCA pp. 58-60]; the gloms as nested hulls above the baseline labelled by type or dubbing [M86 p. 69], the islands of order [FCCA pp. 58-60], pseudo-gloms dotted on the top-down layer [M86 pp. 73, 77], proposals as markers [M86 p. 66], burst, dissolved and blasted gloms fading [M86 pp. 69-70, 92]; click to select in the shared selection; the four layer actions; tooltips in `show`'s format; zoom, pan, fit; `render_png` (G-021 to G-026). |

| `taskrack_view.py` | The taskrack pane, P6 (item 07), after Numbo's `coderack_view.py` and `log_view.py`: the chosen task on a band with its chance, grey when stale [M86 p. 42]; the cleaning banner (DECISION D-010); the waiting tasks as rows by type, coloured by arena (DECISION D-057), with a bar of their share of the weight (DECISION D-007), each row opening to list its tasks; the history of chosen tasks as a virtualised list with the tallies since the last term; the arena filter; a click selects the structures a task names (G-025). | `hypothesis_view.py` | The hypothesis pane, P4 (item 09): the form large with its source [M86 pp. 79, 125, 166]; the form as Ch. 1 Figures 1-2's ovals with the first values beside the top one and each oval's box in its tooltip [M86 pp. 16-17, 50-53, 83], a click lighting the terms it gives; the validity as a number and a bar with the guess threshold's mark [M86 pp. 81, 168]; the predictions of a fresh box over the terms entered, the refuting term red with what was predicted; a list with the episode's casts and their postdiction [M86 pp. 125, 174], the box tree's records with the run's own names behind a "Box tree" button [M86 p. 83], and a private box to Hit and Reset, the appendix's `show-box` [M86 p. 161] (G-033 to G-035). |

| `reformulation_view.py` | The reformulation pane, P7 (item 11): stage two as a storyboard, one episode per refutation with its cards in the order of pp. 87-126 (the refutation, the freeze-dried form, the gnoths set, the slip-check, the Reformulator's moves and PROGRAM, each gnoth operation as a before/after bracket picture with the glom names under the groups [M86 pp. 110-120], the Bond-assessor, the casts, the candidate or the failure, the blast [M86 pp. 92, 124-126]); the freeze-dried list [M86 p. 91] and the threshold plot [M86 p. 105]; "In series" stacks the parses as p. 120 does; a click on a card seeks, a hovered card selects what it names, episodes fold; the pane raises itself at the first refutation (G-039 to G-041). |
| `platoplasm_view.py` | The platoplasm pane, P5 (item 10): the eight ideal types as boxes by category with their format, strictness and the counts of manifestations and actualizations [M86 pp. 54-55, 59, 90, 99], the directed s-links as arrows labelled with their slipperiness (DECISION D-038) [M86 p. 90], the reigning class crowned with the threshold [M86 pp. 99, 105], the ideal atoms as a strip [M86 pp. 48-49], a list with the reigning class's pulls and pushes [M86 p. 91] and the slip-check as it happens [M86 pp. 103-105, 176], the crown travelling the s-link on a class change [M86 p. 174], the "Links" button for the manifestation and actualization lines [M86 p. 45]; a click selects a type's gloms and gnoths (G-025). | `timeline.py` | The timeline toolbar, P10 (item 12), after Numbo's `timeline.py`: the slider over the history, back and forward, the go-to box (`term 5`, `clock 812`, `event 402`), and under the slider the marker strip (term ticks [M86 p. 164], guesses green `ok` or red `no` [M86 pp. 166, 175], refutations [M86 p. 168], slips [M86 p. 174], blasts [M86 pp. 75, 92]) and the parse strip, the parse at each term boundary [M86 p. 174], the time-lapse of Figure 1-1 and the mountain chain [FCCA pp. 41, 64-65]; a click seeks; disabled while a run is live (G-042, G-043). |
| `log_view.py` | The event log pane, P9 (item 12), Numbo's `log_view.py` adopted: one row per event (index, clock, kind, fields) painted only in view, the Kinds menu by kind and by group, Follow, double-click or Enter to seek; Session > Save events (JSON lines)… writes the history as `python3 -m seekwhence --json` does (G-044, G-045). |
| `controls.py` | The controls pane, P11 (item 14), Numbo's `controls.py` adopted: the preset list (the three appendix runs [M86 pp. 164-179], Custom, every sequence of `seekwhence.sequences` with its citation and reported result [M86 pp. 6-7, 145-147]), the free terms, the seed with Random, the two caps (DECISION D-011), the answering mode (interactive, scripted, continuation judge, DECISION D-070), the trace (DECISION D-055), the seven run buttons and the speed slider over `DELAYS`; input errors under the buttons; off during a replay (G-048, G-049). |
| `config_view.py` | The configuration pane, P13 (item 15), a dock hidden by default and opened by Edit > Configuration: every `Config` field as an editable table (a spin box, a decimal spin box, a check box or a line of literal by kind, an editor under the table for the long tables), its default, its source tags and decision numbers with the decisions' headings on hover, its comment, Reset per field and Reset all, a filter; edits apply to the next run only; the controls' caps and trace are shown and edited in the controls pane; a replay shows the file's configuration read-only (ANSWERS.md 5; G-050, G-051). |
| `placeholder.py` | `PanePlaceholder`, the view a dock held until its pane was built (counts events and paints) (G-011). Every pane is built now; it stays for a dock an item may add. |
| `default_layout.json` | The repository's default dock arrangement, shipped with the code (ANSWERS.md 8; G-010). View > Save layout as default for this code… rewrites it; commit it to share it. |
| `headless.py` | The determinism contract, `run(config, seed, lines, answers, window=False)`: a scripted run through the tap, the event log and the Qt-free run controller, or (`window=True`) through the real `MainWindow` offscreen and its own controller. |
| `models/` | The Qt-free models (G-001): the history (item 01), the answerer, the presets and the layout file (item 02), then one per pane. |
| `models/snapshot.py` | `snapshot(world)`: the `World` read into frozen dataclasses of plain data, one section per level, sections shared with the previous snapshot when unchanged, immutable records cached per run (G-003). Reads attributes and the pure methods only. |
| `models/tap.py` | The observer: every event into the event log, and a snapshot at every task boundary while the run is short, keyframes after, always at the prompt, the guess, the answer and the end (G-003, G-004). |
| `models/event_log.py` | The history, as Numbo's `EventLog`: events, counts, rows, lookups by term, clock and index, the snapshots and `state_at(index)` (G-005); the kinds by group and the lock on the rows (G-044). |
| `models/answerer.py` | The `InteractiveAnswerer`: the engine's guess, through the `answerer` hook on the worker, blocking until the user's OK or No (G-008). |
| `models/presets.py` | `RunInputs` (preset, config, seed, lines, answers, interactive, and the `continuation` a judged run's guesses are checked against), the three appendix presets, the interactive run (`interactive_inputs`) and the catalogue's sequences fed as the bench feeds them (`sequence_inputs`, 16 terms, DECISION D-070) (G-009, G-015, G-048). |
| `models/guide.py` | The help page's model (item 16; G-055): the pane-to-pages table of `docs/gui/panes.md` read into `GuideRow`s (`read_guide`, `parse_guide`), `guide_lines` and `guide_html` for Help > What am I looking at?, with P5 and P6 marked GUI design, not Meredith's (ANSWERS.md 4). |
| `models/config_table.py` | The configuration pane's model: `read_fields` (one `FieldNote` per `Config` field from the comments of `config.py`, read with `tokenize`: kind, default, tags, decisions, comment, section), `decision_titles` from DECISIONS.md, `conform` and `parse_text` (every value checked against its field's type, nested tables included, `ConfigError` otherwise), `ConfigTable` (the edits as a dict, `config()` = `dataclasses.replace(Config(), **edits)`, `rows(shown)`, reset, the `config/edits` settings round trip), `filter_rows`, `row_tooltip` (G-050, G-051). |
| `models/controls.py` | The controls pane's model: `DELAYS` and `delay_text` (Numbo's), the preset `catalogue()` with each entry's citation and reported result, the parsers (seed, caps, terms) with `InputError`, `ControlValues` (every control as text) with `run_inputs` to a `RunInputs`, `values_of` back, and the `controls/*` settings round trip checking every value; `random_seed` from Python's `random` (G-048, G-049). |
| `models/sequence.py` | `SequenceModel`: the terms with their fate under the hypothesis in force, the fresh hypothesis's block as ghost cells, the guess pending and the answer badges, from the events alone (G-014). |
| `models/term_feed.py` | `TermFeed`: the stream an interactive run's `Console` reads its lines from; the GUI puts terms in, the worker's `readline` blocks until one comes; close and cancel (G-015). |
| `models/run_stats.py` | `RunStats`: the stats pane's numbers from the events and the snapshot; `finish(outcome)` takes the summary (G-016). |
| `models/commands.py` | `command_lines(snapshot, name)`: `show`, `show-hypothesis`, `show-seq` and `show-parse` rebuilt from a snapshot exactly as `Session.command_lines` prints them (G-017). |
| `models/cytoplasm.py` | `CytoplasmModel`: the glints, sparks, bonds and gloms in force (the snapshot plus the creation, dubbing and destruction events since it), the bond values from the gnoths, the proposals in flight from the rack and the task events, the ghosts of vanished sparks, bonds and gloms, the layers and the shared selection; the pure `layout` and the style, label and tooltip functions (G-021 to G-026). |
| `models/taskrack.py` | `TaskrackModel`: the rack as a shadow kept from the `posted`, `chosen` and `cleaned` events and corrected by every snapshot, the chosen task with its chance at the draw and its staleness, the cleanings, the history and the tallies since the last term; the pure `rack_lines` and the text functions (G-027 to G-029). |
| `models/socratoplasm.py` | `SocratoplasmModel`: the gnoths' cards (the snapshot's records plus the gnoth events since it, the fields of a card touched by a silent task waiting for the next task boundary), the catchall, the parse, the template with its check line and fate from its events, ghosts of gnoths gone, the selection of a gnoth with its glom; the pure `gnoth_boxes`, `pane_lines`, `show_block` and the text functions (G-030 to G-032). |
| `models/hypothesis.py` | `HypothesisModel`: the form, its source, the validity, the refutation and the episode's candidates from the events, the box tree's records from the snapshot taken at the event (a fresh tree with provisional names in between), the predictions and the oval tree's values from fresh boxes, the private `PrivateBox`; the pure `form_tree`, `node_ranges`, `predictions`, `pane_lines` and the text functions (G-033 to G-035). |
| `models/reformulation.py` | `ReformulationModel`: the episodes and their cards from the events, each event on exactly one card; the operation pictures read off a `SocratoplasmModel` of its own; the freeze-dried list, the reigning class and the threshold from the snapshot, with the threshold's course per episode; the pure `picture`, `picture_lines`, `bracket_text`, `series_table`, `series_lines`, `pane_lines` and the text functions (G-039 to G-041). |
| `models/platoplasm.py` | `PlatoplasmModel`: the eight types with their manifestations and actualizations from the snapshot and the `glint`, `dubbed`, `gnoth`, `gnoth-filled`, `gnoths-set`, `dissolved`, `blast` and `blasted` events since it, the s-link, pull and push tables from the run's configuration (`start`), the reigning class and threshold, the slip-check's figures from its event, the crown's travel over `CROWN_MOVE` events after `class-changed`, the atoms, the links layer and the shared selection; the pure `layout`, `check_lines`, `change_text`, `scout_text` and the style and tooltip functions (G-036 to G-038). |
| `models/timeline.py` | `TimelineModel`: the slider's markers (terms, guesses with their verdict, refutations, slips, blasts) and the parse at each term boundary, scanned once per event from the history; the pure `marker_x`, `index_at`, `parse_cells`, `marker_style`, `marker_tooltip`, `parse_goto` and `goto_index` (G-042, G-043). |
| `models/selection.py` | `Selection`: the selection shared by every pane (kind, name, the members it implies, listeners); a glom selects its glints (G-025). |
| `models/console.py` | `ConsoleModel`: two `Trace`s (verbose and terse, with prompts) fed every event, each line tagged with its event's index, plus the lines the session writes at an ask (command echoes and their output from the snapshot, the hint, `bye`, the empty prompt) played from the run's input lines; the summary at the end; append-only, so a seek only moves the cursor; find (G-018, G-019). |
| `models/layout_file.py` | The default layout file's codec: `LayoutRecord`, `encode`, `decode`, `read_layout`, `write_layout` (G-010). |
| `models/run_controller.py` | Numbo's `RunController`: the session on a worker thread behind the gate (pause, deliver, acknowledge, delay); step, step task, step term, play, run to end, stop (G-005). |
| `models/session_file.py` | Session files (item 13), Numbo's `session.py` with a run line: `RunLine`, `SessionRecorder`, `write_session`, `read_session` (a `SessionFile`, or `SessionError` with the path and line), `config_pairs`/`config_from_pairs`, `encode_event`/`decode_event`, `run_line_of`, `run_inputs`; the replay's `ReplayChecker`, `Divergence`, `TraceMismatch` and the Qt-free `replay` through a `RunController` (G-046, G-047). |

The user guide, pane by pane with the thesis pages behind each, is
[`docs/gui/panes.md`](../../docs/gui/panes.md) (item 17; Help > "What am I looking at?"
shows its table). The screenshots of the guide and of `python/README.md`,
`docs/gui-window.png`, `docs/gui-cytoplasm.png`, `docs/gui-reformulation.png` and
`docs/gui-platoplasm.png`, are rendered offscreen by `scripts/docs_screenshots.py` from
