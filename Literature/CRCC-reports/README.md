# CRCC reports and papers, recovered from the Wayback Machine

Everything the Center for Research on Concepts and Cognition (CRCC, Indiana University) once
served from `http://www.cogsci.indiana.edu/pub/`, as the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine
captured it between 2004 and 2015; the site itself is offline (2026). Recovered on
2026-10-18 for FARGonautica from the captures listed below (`https://web.archive.org/web/<capture>/http://www.cogsci.indiana.edu/pub/<file>`).
PostScript files (`.ps`, `.ps.Z`, `.ps.gz`) were converted to PDF with Ghostscript's
`ps2pdf`; PDFs were kept as served. File names are the site's, with the extension changed.
Two files already in [`Ph.D. Theses/`](Ph.D.%20Theses/) (Meredith's Seek-Whence thesis, as
Technical Report 214, and Mahabal's Seqsee thesis) were byte-identical and are not repeated
here; McGraw's thesis is kept once (the 600 dpi copy).

Several are scans with no text layer (noted). Most are unpublished CRCC technical reports or
preprints that the center made public itself; the published ones name their venue.
